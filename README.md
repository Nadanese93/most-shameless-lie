A micro entry for the make.fun bounty "The Most Shameless Lie", competing for a 2 USDC prize.

## Entry

> I paid the rent.

Four words. Sixteen characters. One shameless lie, told with a straight face.

## How it works

`main.py` validates the entry (1–5 words, 1–88 characters, not blank) and writes it atomically to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` as a single line with no trailing newline.
